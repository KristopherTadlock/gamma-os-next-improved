PACK-IN GAMES
=============

gamma-os-next-improved bundles one game per platform. Each game's rights
holder explicitly allows it to be redistributed, and none uses another
company's characters, names, or assets. Each game is installed with its full
license text next to it (<game file>.license.txt).

None of these games has been tested on the RG Cube yet.


GAMES
-----

Game Boy
  Big2Small, by Matthew D. Steele
  A 40-level puzzle game.
  License: GPL-3.0. Art and music derived from CC0/public-domain sources.
  Source: https://github.com/mdsteele/big2small

Game Boy Color
  Tobu Tobu Girl Deluxe, by Tangram Games (music by potato-tan)
  An arcade high-score game: bounce upward by dashing into enemies.
  License: code MIT; art, text, sound and music CC BY 4.0.
  Source: https://github.com/SimonLarsen/tobutobugirl-dx
  Note: already in GammaOS Next 1.2. The bundled file matches the
  official release exactly.

Game Boy Advance
  The Hat Chooses the Wizard, by Corwin Kuiper and Gwilym Inzani
  A platformer made for the GMTK Game Jam 2021.
  License: MPL-2.0. Music "Sylvan Waltz" by Otto Halmén, CC BY 3.0.
  Source: https://github.com/agbrs/agb

Nintendo DS
  AngunaDS, by Nathan Tolbert (art by Chris Hildenbrand)
  An action-adventure game with 5 dungeons.
  License: "Anguna may be distributed freely in binary form, as long as the
  text of this file is made available with it." Source code is MIT.
  Source: https://bitbucket.org/gauauu/angunads
  Note: 2008 homebrew that saves to the SD card; test saving in WatermelonDS.

GameCube
  Super Methane Brothers, by Mark Page (GameCube port by carstene1ns)
  A 100-level action platformer for 1 or 2 players: trap enemies in gas
  and throw them. Originally a 1993 Amiga game by Apache Software.
  License: GPL-2.0. The author released it with Apache Software's
  permission; graphics, music and sound are by the original team.
  Source: https://github.com/carstene1ns/super-methane-brothers
  Note: the publisher's permission is documented only in the author's
  own statement.

Wii
  chro.mono, by Thomas Perl (Wii port by Alberto Mardegan)
  A physics puzzle game: drag colored spheres to recolor the others.
  License: GPL-2.0 or later. Music CC0, sound effects CC BY 3.0 / CC0,
  font SIL Open Font License; all credited in the game.
  Source: https://github.com/mardy/chromono
  Note: uses the Wii Remote pointer; map it to a stick or touch in Dolphin.

Nintendo 3DS
  Anarch, by Miloslav Číž (drummyfish); 3DS port by blitzdoughnuts
  A 90s-style first-person shooter.
  License: CC0 plus a waiver of all rights. The author made all its
  graphics, sounds and music himself.
  Source: https://gitlab.com/drummyfish/anarch
  Note: shooter violence and anti-capitalist messages in the game text.
  The port's author warns audio can be choppy and suggests turning it off.

Xbox
  Second Reality, by Future Crew (Xbox port by kooscode, v1.0.1)
  The 1993 PC demo that won Assembly '93: real-time 3D effects set to a
  tracker soundtrack. A demo to watch, not a game.
  License: Unlicense (public domain). Future Crew released the source and
  data, graphics and music included, under the Unlicense in 2013; the port
  uses the same license. Its menus use the standard IBM VGA text-mode font.
  Source: https://github.com/kooscode/SecondReality
  Note: the port is new (November 2026) and has been tested on a real Xbox
  and in xemu, not in hakuX.

ScummVM
  Beneath a Steel Sky, by Revolution Software (CD version 1.2)
  A 1994 science-fiction point-and-click adventure with full speech;
  backgrounds by comic artist Dave Gibbons.
  License: freeware. "You may distribute this game for free on any medium"
  and "in aggregate as part of a larger & possibly commercial software
  distribution (such as a Linux distribution...)", keeping its readme and
  copyright notices intact. All game content is (C) Revolution Software.
  Source: https://www.scummvm.org/games/#games-sky
  Note: installed into ScummVM's own storage (with its readme), because
  ScummVM can't read /sdcard/ROMs without the user granting access. Daijisho
  starts it through ROMs/scummvm/Beneath a Steel Sky.dpt. About 73 MB.

NES
  Lala the Magical, by The Mojon Twins
  A platformer: an apprentice magician conjures platforms to collect
  three Power Gems.
  License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (non-commercial).
  Source: https://www.mojontwins.com/juegos_mojonos/lala-the-magical/

SNES
  Super Sudoku, by Raphaël Assenat
  Sudoku with 400 puzzles.
  License: MIT.
  Source: https://github.com/raphnet/super_sudoku

Virtual Boy
  VUEngine Platformer Demo, by Jorge Eremiev and Christian Radke
  A 3D platformer demo: one level with 64 coins and secrets.
  License: MIT.
  Source: https://github.com/VUEngine/VUEngine-Platformer-Demo

Master System
  Gravity Beam: Master Gaiden, by Mathew Carr
  A cave-flying game. Code, art, music and sound all by the author.
  License: BSD-3-Clause.
  Source: https://www.smspower.org/Homebrew/GravityBeamMasterGaiden-SMS

Game Gear
  Waternet, by Willems Davy
  A puzzle game: rotate pipes to connect the water network.
  License: MIT. Graphics and music by the author.
  Source: https://github.com/joyrider3774/waternet

Genesis / Mega Drive
  Miniplanets REMIX, by Javier Degirolmo (Sik)
  A platformer across tiny 3D planets: jump obstacles and collect keys.
  License: zlib.
  Source: https://github.com/sikthehedgehog/miniplanets
  Note: already in GammaOS Next 1.2. The bundled file matches the
  official release exactly.

Sega 32X
  Dungeons of Noudar 3D, 32X port by Haroldo O. Pinheiro of Daniel
  Monteiro's game
  A turn-based 3D dungeon crawler with all 8 maps of the original.
  License: BSD-2-Clause.
  Source: https://github.com/haroldo-ok/noudar-32x
  Note: a 2026 port at version 0.0.2; expect rough edges.

Dreamcast
  Demon Bazooka, by Rich Stokes
  An arena shooter. All graphics and sound are generated by its own code.
  License: MIT.
  Source: https://github.com/richstokes/dreamcast-homebrew
  Note: mirrored in this repo's releases (game-mirror-1), because the author
  rebuilds the original download on every change.

PC Engine / TurboGrafx-16
  Worm, by Andy McCall
  A snake game with flowers and spiders.
  License: CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial).
  Source: https://github.com/andymccall/worm (download: andymccall.itch.io/worm)
  Note: marked "in development" by its author. Mirrored in this repo's
  releases (game-mirror-1), because the original is only on itch.io.

Neo Geo Pocket Color
  Stargunner, by Tixul
  A space shooter demo built on the author's NGPCraft engine.
  License: MIT ("Copyright (c) 2026 NGPC Template Contributors"). Its art
  and sound are in the same repo with no other credits.
  Source: https://github.com/Tixul/Stargunner
  Note: its author calls it a demo.

PSP
  KłełeAtoms, by Nightwolf-47
  A chain-reaction strategy game for 1 to 4 players, with computer opponents.
  License: MIT. Uses the DejaVu font.
  Source: https://github.com/Nightwolf-47/KleleAtoms-PSP

PICO-8
  Cherry Bomb, by Krystian Majewski (music by Sebastian Haßler)
  A 9-wave space shooter.
  License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial).
  Source: https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?pid=cherrybomb

Atari 2600
  2048 2600, by Carlos Duarte do Nascimento
  The 2048 sliding-number puzzle, for 1 or 2 players.
  License: MIT (the original 2048 is MIT too).
  Source: https://github.com/chesterbr/2048-2600

Atari 5200
  Curse of the Lost Miner, by BAHA Software (Michael Kalouš)
  A cave platformer with 13+ caves.
  License: public domain.
  Source: https://github.com/baktragh/culomin_sources
  Note: boots through the 5200 BIOS screen, which the emulator replaces
  with its own built-in BIOS; test it.

Atari 7800
  robotfindskitten, by Thomas Mathys
  A "zen simulation": explore the screen to find the kitten.
  License: GPL-2.0 or later.
  Source: https://robotfindskitten.org/download/Atari7800/


PLATFORMS WITHOUT A GAME
------------------------

WonderSwan / WonderSwan Color
  Snail Maze+ adapts Sega's Snail Maze, Wonder Breakanoid is an unfinished
  Arkanoid clone, and Inufuto's games have no license.
Nintendo 64
  Games built with Nintendo's SDK contain Nintendo's code. The other games
  found show the Nintendo 64 logo or use music of unverified origin.
PlayStation
  The homebrew found has no license, uses Sony's SDK, or copies other games.
3DO
  The one licensed game includes the 3DO Company's operating system files.
Arcade
  The MAME project's free ROMs may only be distributed from mamedev.org.
  Users can download them there: https://www.mamedev.org/roms/
