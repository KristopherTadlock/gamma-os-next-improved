PACK-IN GAMES
=============

Thimsy OS bundles one game per platform. Each game's rights holder
explicitly allows it to be redistributed, and none uses another
company's characters, names, or assets. Each game is installed with its
license notice next to it (<game file>.license.txt, or LICENSE.txt in the
game's folder): the full license text, or for Creative Commons licenses a
link to it, plus the notices of libraries built into the game.

Only Anarch (3DS) has been confirmed to run on the RG Cube so far.

BOX ART AND DETAILS
-------------------

Each game is installed with its description, developer, publisher, release
date, genre and number of players, and with box art, a screenshot, its title
screen and a marquee (logo) where there is one, in /sdcard/ES-DE where the
ES-DE theme reads them (mods/pack-in-media.txt). Each image is under its
game's license.

  Downloaded from the game's own project: the screenshots and title screens
  of the other games (Curse of the Lost Miner's is kept in pack-in-art, as
  its author's site is often unreachable), the VUEngine Platformer Demo logo, and the 2048 2600
  cartridge label (by Chester). Nothing comes from game databases, and art
  with other companies' marks or by third parties was left out.
  Captured from the bundled ROMs: the screenshots and title screens of Tobu
  Tobu Girl Deluxe, Super Sudoku, Waternet, Miniplanets REMIX, Worm, Blimp
  Chicken DS and Cherry Bomb.
  Made for this project: the box art of every game but 2048 2600 and
  Cherry Bomb (whose cartridge is its picture), drawn in the style of each
  console's boxes around the game's own pictures, with no console or
  publisher logos; and the title marquees.

Vacpyre, chro.mono, Demon Bazooka and Stargunner have no in-game screenshot:
the first three need emulators that can't run without a display, and
Stargunner's pictures use a third party's art.


PLATFORMS WITHOUT A GAME
------------------------

Xbox
  Second Reality (Future Crew's 1993 demo, Xbox port by kooscode;
  Unlicense) didn't run in hakuX on the RG Cube.
WonderSwan / WonderSwan Color
  Snail Maze+ adapts Sega's Snail Maze, Wonder Breakanoid is an unfinished
  Arkanoid clone, and Inufuto's games have no license.
Nintendo 64
  Games built with Nintendo's SDK contain Nintendo's code. The other games
  found show the Nintendo 64 logo or use music of unverified origin.
PlayStation
  The homebrew found has no license, uses Sony's SDK, or copies other games.
3DO
  The one licensed game includes the 3DO Company's operating system files.
Arcade
  The MAME project's free ROMs may only be distributed from mamedev.org.
  Users can download them there: https://www.mamedev.org/roms/
